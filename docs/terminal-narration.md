# West Gate Terminal: narration

The script as recorded: `public/speeches/T1–T8.mp3` (clip ID = filename). The subtitles follow the recordings' timestamped transcripts (`tools/terminal-transcripts.json`), which word some lines differently (numerals, "Hey, it's Sam."). The terminal itself: `docs/terminal.md`.

## Voices

| Voice | Who | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **NARRATOR** | The documentary voice; Archive only | Warm, unhurried storyteller. Lets the short lines land. |
| **TERMINAL** | The Reckoner, speaking as the system | Calm, clipped, precise, slightly dry. Operational, not chatty. |
| **PRESIDENT** | President Clara Hayes, Centauri Accord | Formal and measured, with real warmth under it. |
| **SAM** | Your friend back on Earth | Casual, affectionate, a little awkward talking to a recorder. |
| **GENERAL** | General Daniel Ward, Accord Frontier Command | Low, economical, used to being obeyed; courteous, not warm. Speaks in short orders. |
| **MAYA** | Your girlfriend back on Earth | Easy, intimate, unscripted: talking into her phone at home, late, in a quiet room (clean, no background noise). Little laughs, trailing off, then sincere. |

Pronunciation: Alpha Centauri *AL-fa sen-TOR-ee*.

## Clips

**T1 · Boot** · TERMINAL · ~12 s
> Terminal awake. Good morning, Guardian. It's noon, as always.
> All systems are running. Use the arrows to choose, and Enter to open.

**T2 · Archive · The story** · NARRATOR · ~60 s
> It began with something small enough to throw.
> In twenty sixty-one, a handful of seeds left the Sun on sails of light. Each one was a forge and a memory, and nothing else. They fell toward Alpha Centauri for twenty-two years, and most of them made it.
> They found rock. They made tools out of the rock, and tools out of the tools, and they were not built to stop. For forty years they took the system apart, asteroid by asteroid, moon by moon, and laid it down in plates around the star, until the plates met.
> In twenty-one twenty-seven, the sphere closed. Two million kilometres across. A star with a roof.
> On the inside, in one small pocket of air, they built a city to keep watch over it: a field of golden machines, a road, a house.
> Then it needed someone to live there.
> That's where you come in.

**T3 · Control · Status** · TERMINAL · ~25 s
> Compound status. All precincts nominal. The Veil is holding at normal load. Band spin is steady, and the Gyre agrees. The Cold Hold is full.
> Craft traffic is on schedule, one every thirty seconds through the East Lock.
> No faults. No alerts.
> One standing order in the house: the kitchen kettle, on. No requester on file.

**T4 · Comms · From the President** · PRESIDENT · ~25 s

> The Assembly chose you to keep watch where none of us can. You won't need to do much. That's the point. Be there. Look after it.
> And write to us. We'll be listening.
