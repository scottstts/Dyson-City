# UI

Code: `src/ui/`, styles in `src/style.css`. Review captures: `node tools/ui-shots.mjs --dist <vite build>` (loading, ready, paused, paused-muted; it also asserts that pause holds the frame loop).

## Entry screen (`entryScreen.ts`)

- **Progress is the sphere, not a bar.** The logo's sphere is one model (`sphereFrame.ts`): the band ring, six hub collars where the spines end on it, six spines, two latitude rings bounding the open bands, two polar collars, and riveted, plated gores standing clear of the spines (narrow slots), cut low either side of the front spine (wide windows onto the star). Orthographic from 12° above the band. Boot lights the 18 members in that order; no stage names or percentages are shown (`fatal.stage` still tracks them for the error panel).
- **Illustrated offline, lit live.** `node tools/sphere-art.mjs` ray traces the model (16 spp, worker threads, ~3 s) into `public/glyph/`: `front` (the outside), `back` (the star and the far shell's lit inner face; alpha is the shell's inner disc, so the cavity is opaque) and `glow` (the star's spill onto edges facing an opening, the gores' coolant seams, the hub lenses; alpha is the outside's coverage, colour × alpha the tone-mapped difference, so front + glow is exact at full heat). The page stacks a bloom, a black cavity disc, `back`, `front`, `glow` (plus-lighter), then the coolant lines as SVG from the same model. `--heat` (the lit fraction) brings up `back` over the black disc, `glow` and the bloom. Re-run the tool after changing the model; `tests/ui.test.ts` checks every coolant line lands on the structure the art draws under it.
- **No layer is drawn on black.** Every image is transparent past the sphere and the glyph is always its own compositing group (`isolation: isolate`). A layer authored as light on black and added with `plus-lighter` turns into a dark square whenever an ancestor's opacity or transform isolates it (the fade in, the ready flare). `tools/ui-shots.mjs` holds the glyph at 0.6 opacity and checks its corner against the bare key art.
- **Compositor-safe.** The bloom's breathing and the key art's drift are transform animations, so they keep moving while boot blocks the main thread; nothing on the screen runs per-frame JS.
- **Key art.** One of the game's own renders (`keyArt.ts`, `public/keyart/`, 2560×1440 progressive JPEG ≤ 500 KB) per visit, drifting slowly under a dark tint; it fades in once decoded and out when the world is ready. To refresh: `node tools/render.mjs --dist <build> --size 2560x1440 --frames 150 --one-boot <spots>`, then encode with `magick <png> -strip -quality 80 -interlace JPEG`.
- **Chatter, not explanation.** One overheard line at a time, low left, tagged with where it was heard (`chatter.ts`): short tips and talk in the voice of the people who keep the place. Never a description of the world ("a city on…"). Lines are ≤64 characters, shuffled so none repeats before all have shown, and stop once the world is ready (`tests/ui.test.ts`).
- **Ready** dissolves the glyph into the live world behind it; the name, a breathing "Enter" and a key-cap row (walk/run/use/pause) remain. The whole screen is the click target. A soft local shade keeps the type legible against the bright apron.
- **Failures** reuse the screen: the frame goes dark and the message and diagnostics sit below it.

## Pause (`pauseMenu.ts`)

- **The world stops.** Losing pointer lock while playing freezes the loop (`Game.freeze`: the animation loop is removed, so nothing advances or renders and the GPU idles). The canvas keeps the last frame. A resize while paused re-renders that same instant once with `dt = 0`, because a resized canvas is cleared. Resuming restarts the loop with no catch-up step.
- **Only sound is settable.** Field of view (`EYE_FOV` in `player/controller.ts`) and mouse sensitivity are fixed. The menu holds a volume line and a mute; a click off the controls or Resume re-locks the pointer.
- **Volume** (`audioPrefs.ts`): 50 % is the mix as authored (`LEVEL.master`). Below that the taper is squared (25 % ≈ −12 dB), above it linear to +6 dB at 100 %, which the engine's output limiter absorbs. Mute sets the gain to 0 rather than suspending the context, so one-shots scheduled while muted never pile up for later. Moving the slider unmutes. Both are stored in `localStorage` (`dyson.audio.v1`); the retired `dyson.settings.v1` key is removed on load.
