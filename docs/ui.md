# UI

Code: `src/ui/`, styles in `src/style.css`. Review captures: `node tools/ui-shots.mjs --dist <vite build>` (loading, ready, paused, paused-muted; it also asserts that pause holds the frame loop).

## Entry screen (`entryScreen.ts`)

- **Progress is the sphere, not a bar.** The logo's sphere is one model (`sphereFrame.ts`): the band ring, six hub collars where the spines end on it, six spines, two latitude rings bounding the open bands, two polar collars, and riveted, plated gores standing clear of the spines (narrow slots), cut low either side of the front spine (wide windows onto the star). Orthographic from 12° above the band. Boot lights the 17 members in that order; no stage names or percentages are shown (`fatal.stage` still tracks them for the error panel). It is an icon (68 CSS px) leading the overheard line in the lower-left corner, not a centrepiece.
- **Illustrated offline, lit live.** `node tools/sphere-art.mjs` ray traces the model (36 spp, worker threads, ~8 s) at three times the icon's CSS size into `public/glyph/`, in two parts: the sphere with the belt (the band and the hub collars it runs through) left out, and the belt alone. The first gives `front` (the outside), `back` (the star and the far shell's lit inner face; alpha is the shell's inner disc, so the cavity is opaque) and `glow` (the star's spill onto edges facing an opening, the gores' coolant seams; alpha is the outside's coverage, colour × alpha the tone-mapped difference, so front + glow is exact at full heat); the second gives `belt` and `belt-glow` the same way. The page stacks a bloom, a black cavity disc, `back`, `front`, `glow` (plus-lighter), the belt, then the coolant lines as SVG from the same model. `--heat` (the lit fraction) brings up `back` over the black disc, both glows and the bloom. Re-run the tool after changing the model; `tests/ui.test.ts` checks every coolant line lands on the structure the art draws under it, at any turn of the band.
- **Only the band turns**, as in the world (`docs/design.md`): it runs through the fixed hub collars, so the collars, spines and every coolant line (the band's channels run along it) stay put and light member by member as before. The belt is a sprite sheet of the band's turn (`BELT`: 120 frames over the 60° the band's pattern takes to repeat, its grain wrapped on a 60° circle so the loop is seamless; 10 s per period, a full turn a minute), each frame rendered with the collars over the band so they occlude it. One Web Animations transform steps the sheet behind a window, so it turns on the compositor while boot blocks the main thread; it stops on failure and once the screen is hidden. The turn reads at icon size as the band's ribs and grain crawling past the collars. Coolant lines keep a drawn width (non-scaling stroke) and glow in CSS px; at icon size the model's widths would be sub-pixel and break up.
- **No layer is drawn on black.** Every image is transparent past the sphere and the glyph is always its own compositing group (`isolation: isolate`). A layer authored as light on black and added with `plus-lighter` turns into a dark square whenever an ancestor's opacity or transform isolates it (the fades; the belt's stepped sheet is such a group, which is why its glow is added onto its own outside only). `tools/ui-shots.mjs` holds the glyph at 0.6 opacity and checks its corner against the bare key art.
- **Compositor-safe.** The bloom's breathing, the band's turn and the key art's cycle are transform and opacity animations, so they keep moving while boot blocks the main thread; nothing on the screen runs per-frame JS. Once the screen is hidden they stop rather than run behind the game.
- **Key art.** Three stills of the game (`keyArt.ts`, `public/keyart/`: entrance, house, pad; 3344×1882 WebP ≤ 1.5 MB) take turns under a dark tint, 5 s each, starting from a different one per visit. Each closes in slowly (scale 1 → 1.06) the whole 6.2 s it is on screen; the incoming still fades in (1.2 s) over the outgoing one, held opaque beneath it, except at the wrap, where the top layer fades out over the first, so there is never a dip to the tint. One 15 s CSS cycle (`.entry-still`), started once the first still is decoded; one not yet in when its turn comes shows the tint. The art fades out when the world is ready, so its size never blocks entry. Ship the stills as supplied; do not re-encode them to save bytes.
- **Chatter, not explanation.** One overheard line at a time, low left after the sphere icon, tagged with where it was heard (`chatter.ts`): short tips and talk in the voice of the people who keep the place. Never a description of the world ("a city on…"). A new line every 5 s, the stills' cadence (0.6 s fades). Every line is in the DOM, stacked in one cell, and takes its turn by a compositor opacity animation: timers fire late while boot blocks the main thread. Lines are ≤64 characters, shuffled so none repeats before all have shown, and stop once the world is ready (`tests/ui.test.ts`).
- **The name waits for ready.** Loading shows no title; ready fades the corner (icon and line) and the art into the live world behind, and fades in the name at its title size with a breathing "Enter" and a key-cap row (walk/run/use/pause). The whole screen is the click target. A soft local shade keeps the type legible against the bright apron.
- **Failures** reuse the screen: the sphere goes dark in its corner, the band stops, and the message and diagnostics sit in the centre.

## Pause (`pauseMenu.ts`)

- **The world stops.** Losing pointer lock while playing freezes the loop (`Game.freeze`: the animation loop is removed, so nothing advances or renders and the GPU idles). The canvas keeps the last frame. A resize while paused re-renders that same instant once with `dt = 0`, because a resized canvas is cleared. Resuming restarts the loop with no catch-up step.
- **Only sound is settable.** Field of view (`EYE_FOV` in `player/controller.ts`) and mouse sensitivity are fixed. The menu holds a volume line and a mute; a click off the controls or Resume re-locks the pointer.
- **Volume** (`audioPrefs.ts`): 50 % is the mix as authored (`LEVEL.master`). Below that the taper is squared (25 % ≈ −12 dB), above it linear to +6 dB at 100 %, which the engine's output limiter absorbs. Mute sets the gain to 0 rather than suspending the context, so one-shots scheduled while muted never pile up for later. Moving the slider unmutes. Both are stored in `localStorage` (`dyson.audio.v1`); the retired `dyson.settings.v1` key is removed on load.
